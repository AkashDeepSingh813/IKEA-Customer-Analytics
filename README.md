# IKEA Customer Analytics & RFM Segmentation

**Tools:** SQL | Python | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed **1M+ retail transaction records** to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify high-value customer segments, and assess churn risk. Used SQL, Python, and Power BI to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights and identify opportunities for customer retention.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify high-value customers and analyze their revenue contribution.
- Segment customers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics.
- Identify churned customers using a 90-day inactivity threshold.
- Analyze customer distribution, segment performance, and churn trends.

## 🔄 Methodology

- **SQL – Data Preparation:** Cleaned, deduplicated, and prepared transaction data for analysis.
- **Python – RFM Segmentation:** Calculated RFM metrics and classified customers into six segments: Champions, Loyal, At Risk, New, Needs Attention, and Lost. Applied a 90-day inactivity rule to classify churn status.
- **Power BI – Dashboard Development:** Built a three-page interactive dashboard featuring key performance indicators, customer segmentation, revenue analysis, and churn trends. Used DAX for calculations and implemented filters for time periods, countries, customer segments, and churn status.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Customer Churn:** Identified a 50.9% churn rate based on the defined 90-day inactivity rule.
- **Revenue Contribution:** The Champions segment generated approximately **$11.84M in revenue**, highlighting its importance to overall business performance.
- **Customer Retention:** Identified opportunities for targeted retention and re-engagement strategies through segment-level analysis.
- **Interactive Analysis:** Enabled exploration of customer behavior, revenue contribution, and churn trends across different segments and time periods.
